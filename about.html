About Pawpets

Our story

A little shop with a big

Pawpets began in 2018 as a tiny corner store with a simple idea: pets deserve the very best. Today we are a full-service pet-care destination offering food, toys, grooming, boarding, vet care and training under one roof.

Our team of vets, groomers and trainers share one belief – happy pets make happy homes. Whether you have a playful puppy, a curious cat, a gentle rabbit or a chatty bird, we are here to help.
