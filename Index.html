Feeding Guide: How Much Should Your Dog Eat?
Nutrition

Portion size depends on age, weight and activity. Adult dogs usually do best on two measured meals a day. Choose a complete food with real protein as the first ingredient, keep fresh water available, and adjust portions if your dog is gaining or losing weight. Always introduce new food slowly over 7 days to avoid upset tummies.

5 Tips for a Happy, Healthy Indoor Cat
Cat care

Indoor cats need stimulation: a tall scratching post, window perches and 15 minutes of interactive play twice a day keep them fit and calm. Brush weekly to cut down on hairballs, provide a clean litter tray in a quiet spot and book a yearly vet check-up.

Rabbit Basics: Hay, Veggies and Hop Time
Small pets

Hay should make up around 80% of a rabbit's diet, with fresh leafy greens daily and only a small amount of pellets. Rabbits need several hours of exercise outside the hutch and a friend of their own kind makes them happier.

Bringing Home a Bird: Your First Week Checklist
Birds

Give your new bird a spacious cage in a bright, draught-free room. Keep a routine, talk softly, and offer seed mix, fresh fruit and veg. Let the bird settle for a few days before handling and provide perches and safe toys for chewing.
